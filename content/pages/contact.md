If you want to reach the paper, the easiest route is to contact **Hardhy Lester on Discord (@thesaffen)**.

This is the best option for:

* Story tips
* Correction requests
* Public event notices
* Interview offers
* Questions about contributing to the paper

## What To Include

To help the newsroom sort your message quickly, include a short note about:

* Who you are writing as
* What the matter concerns
* Where the event or incident took place
* When it happened or is expected to happen
* Whether the information is public, private, or off the record

## Coverage Requests

If you would like The Lion's Roar to cover an event, include the event name, time, location, and a short description of what attendees can expect.

Advance notice helps, but the paper has been known to appear at the last minute if the rumour sounds interesting enough.

## Corrections

If you believe an article contains a factual error, send the article title or link along with the correction you want reviewed.

The newsroom would rather fix a mistake than let it grow legs.
