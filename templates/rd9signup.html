Before you apply

Roaring Days 9 is shorter than previous years, which means we cannot guarantee a slot for all applicants. Programming will be curated to create a good flow across the weekend, with a balance of genres, tones, and set lengths.

Artists may be offered a showcase set (10–15 min), a standard set (20–30 min), or a feature set (40–45 min), depending on availability and weekend pacing.
